How it works

A small model trained on synthetic graded trials estimates pass chances for three capability tiers. Text signals adjust for complexity. The router picks the cheapest model and effort meeting the stakes target, or the strongest predicted option when none does.

These are prototype estimates, not measured guarantees for your task. Prices and model mappings are bundled snapshots. This demo uses default settings and no personal learning.

Privacy

Runs entirely in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

Your task stays in this page’s memory. The demo makes no provider calls and saves no task history. Loading the page and its bundled data uses your static host.
